More analysis is needed to ensure that tropical forest degeneration and regeneration are consistently reported in carbon cycle models and in national reporting, according to a study funded in part by ESA's Climate Change Initiative Biomass project. The study analysed how tropical forests store carbon under varying conditions and then release it back into the atmosphere.
